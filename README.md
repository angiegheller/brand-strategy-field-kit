# Brand Strategy Field Kit

Practical templates for brand, campaign, audience, creator, and launch strategy.

This is a working library for marketers, founders, creators, and brand teams who need clearer thinking before they spend money, brief a team, launch a campaign, or chase a trend.

## What's Inside

- Brand positioning canvas
- Audience map
- Campaign brief
- Creator partnership brief
- Launch review
- Measurement plan
- Decision memo

## Who This Is For

- Marketers building sharper briefs
- Founders trying to explain their brand
- Creators turning attention into a real offer
- Agencies that need cleaner client thinking
- Brand leaders who want strategy people can actually use

## How To Use This

1. Open the template you need.
2. Duplicate it into your own document.
3. Fill it out with real audience, offer, channel, and measurement context.
4. Treat the final answer as a working decision, not a perfect theory.

## Templates

| Template | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| `brand-positioning-canvas.md` | You need a clear point of view before making content, offers, or campaigns. |
| `audience-map.md` | You need to understand who you are speaking to and what they care about. |
| `campaign-brief.md` | You need to turn an idea into a focused campaign. |
| `creator-partnership-brief.md` | You are evaluating creators, influencers, or collaborators. |
| `launch-review.md` | You want to learn from a launch instead of moving on too fast. |
| `measurement-plan.md` | You need to define what success means before the work begins. |
| `decision-memo.md` | You need to make a recommendation and show the reasoning. |

## Working Rules

- Start with the audience.
- Define the decision before collecting information.
- Separate facts from interpretation.
- Make the tradeoff visible.
- Protect confidential client information.
- Keep the final recommendation human-owned.
